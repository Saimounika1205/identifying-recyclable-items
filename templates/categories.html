♻️ Waste Categories & Recyclability

Learn which types of waste are recyclable, and where you can take them!

🟢 Plastic

✅ Recyclable

Examples: Bottles, Containers, Packaging
Tip: Rinse before recycling.

🟢 Glass

✅ Recyclable

Examples: Jars, Bottles, Windows
Tip: Remove lids before recycling.

🟠 E-Waste

⚠️ Special Handling Required

Examples: Old phones, Batteries, Electronics
Tip: Take to certified e-waste recycling centers.

🔴 Non-Recyclable Waste

❌ Not Recyclable

Examples: Diapers, Sanitary products, Contaminated packaging

🟢 Paper & Cardboard

✅ Recyclable

Examples: Newspapers, Magazines, Office Paper
Tip: Flatten cardboard boxes.

🟢 Organic Waste

✅ Recyclable via Composting

Examples: Food scraps, Garden waste
Tip: Compost at home or community gardens.

Tip: Check local recycling centers for specific rules!
